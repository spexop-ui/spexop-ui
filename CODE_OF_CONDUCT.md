# Code of Conduct

We want Spexop UI discussions and contributions to be welcoming, constructive, and safe.

Be respectful and specific. Assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Do not use harassment, hate speech, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, or repeated unwanted contact. Do not publish another person's private information.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may limit participation when needed to protect the community. Decisions are made case by case and may include a warning or a ban.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately through an established project contact channel. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. For security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
